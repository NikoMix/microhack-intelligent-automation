# Contoso Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year. Unused leave carries over only up to five days and must be used before 31 March of the following year.

## Sick Leave
Employees should notify their manager before 09:30 local time on the first sick day. A medical certificate is required when sick leave exceeds three consecutive working days.

## Parental Leave
Parental leave requests should be submitted at least eight weeks before the planned start date. HR confirms eligibility and records the approved dates in the HR system.
