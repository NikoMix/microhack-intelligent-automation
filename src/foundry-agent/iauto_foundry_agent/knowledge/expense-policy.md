# Contoso Expense Policy

## Eligible Expenses
Employees may claim reasonable business expenses for customer travel, approved training, and project delivery. Receipts are required for every item over 25 EUR. Alcohol, personal entertainment, and commuting from home to the normal office are not reimbursable.

## Approval Thresholds
Expenses up to 250 EUR require manager approval. Expenses from 250.01 EUR to 2,000 EUR require cost-center owner approval. Anything above 2,000 EUR requires Finance approval before purchase.

## Submission Timing
Claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense date. Late claims are returned unless the employee includes a manager-approved exception note.
